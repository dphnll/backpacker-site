For organizers

You prepare the program. Guests know what to expect

For anyone who hosts guests, leads a group or puts together a visit program

  • Guides and tour operators
  • Hospitality managers
  • Business trips and visits
  • Group and event coordinators
  • Trips for family or friends
  1. 1

    Build the program

    Arrival, transfers, stays, meetings, events and departure – by day and by time. Prices, bookings and notes stay with you.

  2. 2

    Share it with participants

    One link to the published program. Inside: times, places, price, what is included and the materials they need.

  3. 3

    Update it as plans change

    A time or place changed? Edit the program. Participants open the current published version from the same link and see when it was updated.

For guests and participants

You’ve landed. The program is already on your phone

The organizer sent a link with everything you need for the coming days

  • Where and when Meetings, transfers and events by day, with times and places
  • What is included Price, what is and isn’t included, what to know in advance
  • Materials Guides, maps and documents the organizer shared with the group

You can view the program from the link without signing up. To join the group and keep the program in your list, you need an email and a name.

For your own trips

Your trip. Your pace

Gather what you’ve found, lay it out by day and keep it at hand on the road

  1. 01

    Collect

    Describe the trip by text or voice and Clavelo builds a draft from your words only. Save finds from websites with the Chrome extension.

    Clavelo Travel Capture for Chrome
  2. 02

    Lay it out

    Every event is a card with time, price, booking and files. Plans changed? Drag the card to another day.

  3. 03

    Use it on the trip

    Tickets, addresses and bookings open from your phone. Totals for each day and the whole trip add up on their own.
