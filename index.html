Voice or text

Describe the trip and get a draft

Describe the trip in your own words, by text or voice. The draft uses only your words and documents. Anything approximate is marked. Review and confirm the draft to create the trip in Backpacker.

Ideas from the browser

Save a travel find to Backpacker

The Chrome extension for desktop saves information available on the current page and sends the find to Ideas. Some sites may not make every detail available; the source link is kept with the idea. Move the idea into a trip when you are ready, or keep it for later.

Install for Chrome

Day by day

A day is a stream of cards

Every event has its own card. Browse the day as a clear sequence of events. Plans changed? Move a card to another day or keep it in Ideas for later.

Event card

Everything you need, together

Time, price, booking link, status and notes stay in one card. Add useful files: a ticket or booking PDF, boarding pass, voucher scan or photo. See what is paid and what is still only an idea without searching through chats.

Budget

Totals update automatically

As you edit and move cards, Backpacker recalculates totals for each day and the whole trip. If the cost is unknown, leave it empty or add an estimate.

Organizer Mode

One current program for the whole group

The organizer manages the trip program. Participants open the current program, its price and conditions, and materials added by the organizer. The organizer’s internal budget is not shared with participants.
